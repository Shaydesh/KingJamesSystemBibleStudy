A’-BEL--ME-HO'-LAH (meadow of dancing). 
A city in Issachar, at the N. of the Jordan Valley, 
and ten or twelve miles S. of Beth-shean; the 
birth-place of Elisha the prophet, son of Shaphat. 
Judg. 7:22; I Ki. 4:12; 19:16. 
A'-BEL--MIZ'-RA-IM (meadow of Egypt). 
A place at the threshing-floor of Atad, “beyond 
Jordan,” probably on the W., between it and 
Hebron, at Beth-hogla, now Ain-Hajla; according 
to others, at El-Haram, near Hebron. See ATAD. 
Gen. 50:11. 
A'-BEL--SH1T'-T1M (meadow of acacia). 
A place in the plains (or deserts) of Moab E. of 
the Jordan, about seven miles off, opposite Jericho, 
and nearly W. of Heshbon. It was the extreme 
N. border of the last encampment of Israel be¬ 
fore passing over the Jordan, B. C. 1451. See 
SHITTIM. Num. 33:49. 
A'-BEZ (white, shining)—R. V., “EbezJ* 
A city in Issachar, near Kishion or Remeth (per¬ 
haps the same as Thebez, now Tubas), near 
Engannin and Shunem. Josh. 19:20. 
A’-BI (Jehovah is father). 
The daughter of Zechariah, wife of Ahaz and 
mother of Hezekiah, kings of Judah; called 
ABIJAH in II Ch. 29:1. II Ki. 18:2.

judge of Israel. With his brother Joel or Vashni,

A'-BI--AL'-BON (father of strength). 
One of David’s mighty men, from Arabah or Beth- 
arabah, a city in the N. of the country of the 
tribe of Judah, adjacent to the territory of the 
tribe of Benjamin. He is called ABIEL in 1 Ch. 
11:32. 11 Sa. 23:31.
